What we can start from

Give us what you have. We help turn it into something real.

The goal is not to sell you another design tool. The goal is to understand the product, make the HMI behavior concrete, and get to a prototype that can be discussed on real embedded hardware.

A practical first review

We do not need everything decided on day one.

The first pass is simply to understand what the HMI should do, what information you already have, and which unknowns matter most. We can then decide whether the right next step is a UI prototype, an architecture review, reference hardware, or a combination.
