# V0.6 Module Commercial & U.S. Import Policy

## Public website rule

- Do **not** publish a fixed U.S. tariff rate for HMI modules.
- Do **not** market a module as having a “7.5% U.S. tariff” unless the exact SKU, country of origin, 8/10-digit HTS classification and current additional duties have been verified for the transaction date.
- The HMI module strategy is positioned around integration speed, software readiness, development resources and product fit — not an assumed tariff advantage.

## Project quotation rule

For U.S. landed-cost discussions, confirm for the exact product configuration:

1. Country of origin.
2. 8- or 10-digit HTS classification.
3. Normal duty rate in the current USITC HTS.
4. Current Section 301 applicability and additional rate using the USTR product search.
5. Any current exclusions, special measures or configuration-specific classification issues.
6. Customs-broker confirmation before using the rate in a customer quotation.

## Why this policy

As of August 2026, U.S. tariff treatment remains classification- and measure-specific. USITC continues to revise the 2026 HTS, while USTR instructs importers to determine the exact HTS subheading before checking Section 301 applicability. A broad “module = 7.5%” assumption is therefore not reliable enough for public marketing or commercial commitments.
