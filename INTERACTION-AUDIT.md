# Open HMI Network v0.3.4.3 — Interaction Consistency Audit

This patch standardizes card interactions across the site.

- Single-destination cards are fully clickable.
- Small card CTA text is no longer the only click target.
- Remaining text CTAs have larger hit areas.
- Non-clickable information cards no longer lift on hover.
- Keyboard focus states are added for clickable cards.
- Dynamic resource, vendor and contributor cards follow the same rule.
- Multi-action contribution cards remain multi-action to avoid ambiguous navigation.
- CSS/JS cache versions are bumped to 0.3.4.3.
