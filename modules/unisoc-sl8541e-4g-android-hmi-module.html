Good starting fit

Discuss this path when the product needs:

  • Connected HMI products where cellular is part of the system concept
  • Android UI with moderate display resolution and mature industry software requirements
  • Equipment terminals, connected controls and other embedded products that benefit from an established 4G platform
  • Projects that need to evaluate a proven platform before committing to new module development
